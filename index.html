Stack Calculator

Type a math expression. One stack turns it into postfix, and a second stack evaluates it. Both are shown step by step.

Step 1: infix to postfix (operator stack)

Operators wait on the stack until an operator that binds less tightly, or a closing bracket, pops them.

Step 2: evaluate postfix (value stack)

Numbers are pushed. An operator pops two values, calculates, and pushes the result. Stacks read left (bottom) to right (top).
